zapper: mpv and FFmpeg
======================

zapper plays video with libmpv, the library form of the mpv media player,
which decodes through FFmpeg. Neither is part of zapper's own code (MIT):
they are separate libraries, each under its own license, loaded by zapper
when it starts. Whether a zapper package contains them depends on the
platform.

  mpv     https://mpv.io
          source: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  FFmpeg  https://ffmpeg.org
          source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources

Both are free software under the GNU GPL or LGPL. mpv is GPL-2.0-or-later
(LGPL-2.1-or-later when built without its GPL parts); FFmpeg is
LGPL-2.1-or-later (GPL-2.0-or-later, or GPL-3.0-or-later, when built with its
GPL or version-3 parts). Which applies is decided by how your copy was built.


Linux (.deb and .rpm)
---------------------

Not included. The packages depend on your distribution's libmpv (libmpv2 or
libmpv1 on Debian and Ubuntu, mpv-libs on Fedora), which your package manager
installs and updates, with FFmpeg's libraries. Their licenses are installed
with them (/usr/share/doc/libmpv2/copyright on Debian and Ubuntu), and their
source is your distribution's source package (`apt source mpv`,
`dnf download --source mpv`).


Windows (installer and MSI)
---------------------------

Included, as libmpv-2.dll beside zapper.exe: the `mpv-dev-x86_64` build
published by the mpv-winbuild-cmake project (the builds mpv's own website
points Windows users to), unmodified:

  https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake/releases

That build compiles mpv with its GPL parts (its default) and FFmpeg with
--enable-gpl and --enable-version3, and links into the DLL the libraries they
use, each under its own license: among them libass, FreeType, HarfBuzz,
FriBidi, dav1d, libvpx, libaom, SVT-AV1, x264, x265, libplacebo, LittleCMS,
OpenSSL, LuaJIT, MuJS, libarchive, libbluray, libdvdnav, Rubber Band, uchardet
and zimg. The DLL as a whole is therefore under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 or later; its text is in GPL-3.0.txt beside this file.

The source of libmpv-2.dll is that of mpv, FFmpeg and the libraries it
contains. The build recipe that made it, with each library's source address,
is the mpv-winbuild-cmake repository at the commit of the release (the
release's tag is its build date, and the archive's name,
mpv-dev-x86_64-<date>-git-<commit>.7z, carries the mpv commit it was built
from). You can replace libmpv-2.dll with any other build of libmpv 2 (from
another mpv-winbuild-cmake release, or one you build yourself): zapper loads
whichever is beside zapper.exe.


macOS
-----

Not included: the macOS port is unfinished, and its builds use the libmpv
installed on the Mac (Homebrew's mpv, for instance), under the terms that copy
came with.


zapper reaches libmpv through the libmpv2 and libmpv2-sys Rust crates
(LGPL-2.1), which are compiled into zapper itself: THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-rust.txt
has their license and where their source is.
